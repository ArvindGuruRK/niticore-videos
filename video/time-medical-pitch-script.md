## 1. The story in one line

> TIME Medical is growing in two markets at once. The rules and the hospital buyers in both are starting
> to ask for proof that its AI is safe and well managed. Niticore helps TIME Medical have that proof
> ready **before** someone asks, instead of scrambling **after**.

The film moves in four steps:

1. **Who you are** – TIME Medical, growing on two tracks (India and Europe)
2. **What's at stake** – the regulatory clock, and, bigger still, lost hospital business
3. **What Niticore is** – one platform for AI governance across both markets
4. **Why now** – build it in today, or retrofit it when a tender or audit is on the line

## 6. Look and feel

- **Same style as the Niticore launch film:** a dark background, with flowing points of light that
  carry each scene into the next. There are no hard cuts.
- **Brand colours:** white text on dark. Green is used only once, for the key phrase in the final
  question. Soft violet is used for the lines of light and the drawings.
- **Type:** Niticore's own fonts; normal sentence case (no ALL-CAPS text).
- **Flags:** India and EU flags appear as small, clean icons beside each market.
- **Hospital names:** shown as plain text only. No hospital logos are used.
- **TIME Medical:** its own logo (supplied by TIME Medical), shown unaltered on a white plate.
- **No traffic-light colours:** no red, amber or green dots to signal good or bad.
