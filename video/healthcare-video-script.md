# Niticore for healthcare: video script

| | |
|---|---|
| **For** | Internal review (manager), then any healthcare prospect |
| **Length** | About 50 seconds |
| **Who it's for** | Hospitals and health systems, pharmaceutical companies, and medical device and equipment makers. No customer is named, so the same film works for every prospect. |
| **Focus** | Introduce Niticore and its AI-agent approach, show the industry's problem, show how Niticore helps (briefly, not a feature tour), and end on why it matters to healthcare companies. |
| **Format** | 16:9 widescreen (email, meetings, LinkedIn). A 9:16 vertical cut can follow if needed. |
| **Voice** | The same narrator as the Niticore launch film. The opening and closing lines are the launch film's own recordings. |
| **Look** | The launch film's style (a dark background, flowing points of light, Niticore's own interface components), with its own healthcare centrepiece: a turning DNA double helix of light, with glossy medicine capsules for the AI systems. |

---

## 1. The story in one line

> AI is already at work across healthcare, but unchecked models, agents and patient data are a risk, and
> regulators and hospitals now ask for proof. Niticore brings every model and agent into one place, with
> risk assessed, people in the loop and evidence ready, so healthcare companies can scale AI with confidence.

## 3. Scene by scene

### Scene 1 · Opening (0:00 – 0:12), unchanged from the launch film

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "The world is accelerating with AI." · "Now, AI needs governance built for what's next." · "Introducing Niticore." |
| **On-screen text** | The world is **accelerating** with AI. · Now, AI needs **governance** built for what's next. |
| **Visual** | A spark unfurls into ribbons of light that speed up; each headline rolls in word by word; the Niticore wordmark builds letter by letter with its two sparkles. |
| **Hands over** | The wordmark's light pours into a DNA double helix. |

### Scene 2 · AI in healthcare: the DNA helix (0:12 – 0:18)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "In healthcare, AI agents now support diagnosis, triage and drug discovery." |
| **On-screen text** | AI is already at work in **healthcare**. · Clinical decision support, triage, diagnostic imaging and drug discovery. |
| **Visual** | The light builds a turning DNA double helix from the bottom up: two strands of light, rungs between them, glossy violet bases. Five glossy two-tone medicine capsules (the AI systems) bud from its bases and orbit it, each labelled: Clinical decision support · Triage agent · Diagnostic imaging · Drug discovery · Patient records. |

### Scene 3 · Governance hasn't kept pace: the problem (0:18 – 0:26)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "But governance hasn't kept pace." · "Models no one validated." · "Agents acting on their own." · "Patient data at risk." |
| **On-screen text** | The four lines stack up as they are spoken. |
| **Bridge** | On "But governance hasn't kept pace." the helix slows and dims and the music holds one chord, before the problems arrive. |
| **Visual** | The helix acts out each risk: it turns amber and flickers (label: *Not validated*); one capsule breaks orbit and drifts away (label: *Agent acting alone*); the helix turns coral and starts to unravel, its bases shaken loose (label: *Patient data at risk*). |

### Scene 4 · Regulators and hospitals ask for proof (0:26 – 0:30)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "And regulators and hospitals now ask for proof." |
| **On-screen text** | Regulators and hospitals now ask for **proof**. |
| **Visual** | Four requests float in around the helix, each tied to it by a fine line: Model documentation · Bias testing · Post-market surveillance · Data-protection assessment. Then the helix coils back, turns violet again, takes the capsules home and dissolves into light. |

### Scene 5 · Niticore: one AI register (0:30 – 0:38)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "Niticore governs every model and agent: risk assessed, people in the loop, evidence ready for every audit." |
| **On-screen text** | Every model and agent, in **one place**. |
| **Visual** | The light lands as a Niticore panel, the *AI register*: the five systems from scene 2 as rows (with their type: Model, Agent, Dataset). Three columns tick green down the rows, each on its spoken words: **Risk assessed** · **People in the loop** · **Evidence ready**. Underneath: *Mapped to* EU AI Act · ISO/IEC 42001. |

> This is the only "how" in the film: three ticks, not a feature tour (as the client asked).

### Scene 6 · Why it matters, then a pause (0:38 – 0:43)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "So you can scale healthcare AI with confidence, and prove it." |
| **On-screen text** | Under "Every model and agent, in **one place**.": So you can scale healthcare AI with confidence, and prove it. |
| **Visual** | The completed register stays on screen. At 0:41 everything holds still for a pause (the music holds one chord), then the register pours into the end card. |

### Scene 7 · End card (0:43 – 0:50), as in the launch film

| | |
|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | "Niticore." · "AI governance, from policy to proof." |
| **On-screen** | The Niticore wordmark with its tagline, *AI Governance. From Policy to Proof.*, then www.niticore.ai typed underneath. |
