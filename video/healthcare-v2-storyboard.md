## 1. What changes in V2, in one glance

| # | Change asked for | What we will do |
|---|------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Show the **real risks** on the DNA | The real risks from the TIME Medical brief ride **on the tablets**: as each risk is spoken, one capsule's name label turns into its risk. |
| 2 | **Labels move left and right** too much | Each label rides centred on top of its own pill and never jumps sides; the orbit slows while the risks are on, and labels step aside when two pills pass close. |
| 3 | Add the **"bigger risk" screen** (hospital buyers and tender list) after the DNA | The screen from the TIME Medical film comes in straight after the DNA: "The bigger risk: losing business before a regulator ever comes knocking.", the hospital buyers and "What a tender can ask for". |
| 4 | **Risk assessed · People in the loop · Evidence ready** as **separate screens**, not one table | After the AI register, each of the three gets its **own screen**, designed for what it shows, one after another. The finale brings back the AI register card with all three columns ticking. |
| 5 | Make it amazing and visually intuitive | Each new screen is built from real Niticore interface components, assembles out of the particles and flows into the next (as in V1). |

Everything else stays as in V1: the opening, the DNA helix and its capsules, "But governance hasn't kept pace.", the pause, and the end card.

### Scene 4 · The real risks, pinned on the DNA (0:20 – 0:30) 🆕

**The idea.** The risks ride on the tablets. As each risk is spoken, one capsule's name label turns into its risk
(in the warning colour), centred on top of the pill and following it as it orbits. The helix changes colour with the
risk, as in V1 (amber, then coral and unravelling).

| Order | Voiceover | Tablet → its label becomes | Helix |
|-------|-----------|----------------------------|-------|
| 1 | "Models no one validated." | Clinical decision support → **Models no one validated** | Turns amber |
| 2 | "Agents acting on their own." | Triage agent (drifts away) → **Agents acting on their own** | Amber |
| 3 | "Patient data at risk." | Patient records → **Patient data at risk** | Turns coral, starts to unravel |
| 4 | — (on screen only) | Diagnostic imaging → **Governance gaps found late** | Coral |
| 5 | — (on screen only) | Drug discovery → **Evidence scattered across spreadsheets** | Coral |
| 6 | "And the regulatory clock is already ticking." 🆕 | The problem lines step away; *And the regulatory clock / is already ticking.* appears on two lines, with two cards under it: **India · DPDP Act: up to ₹250 crore** and **Europe · EU AI Act: 2 August 2027** | A ring of light ticks round the helix once |

The small print from the TIME Medical film goes under the two law labels: *"For certain contraventions. Subject to
the applicable provisions and timelines."*

## 6. Decisions for the manager

| # | Question | Our suggestion |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Who is V2 for?** V1 was a reusable film for any healthcare company. V2 adds India-specific content (Indian hospital names, the DPDP Act). | Treat V2 as the **India healthcare** version. Keep V1 as the general one for other markets. |
| 2 | Show the six **hospital names**, or generic buyer types (for example *Public hospitals · Private hospital chains · State health departments*)? | Names, as in the screenshot, if V2 is for India; generic types if it must be reusable everywhere. |
| 3 | Length about **62 s** (we agreed up to 55 s earlier). | Fine for a sales film. To get under 55 s: drop the "regulatory clock" line and its two labels (−3 s) and shorten each of the three screens by 1 s (−3 s): about 56 s. |
| 4 | The two on-screen-only risks (gaps found late; spreadsheets): keep them silent, or voice them? | Keep them silent: the voice already carries five risk lines. |
| 5 | After the three screens: what is the finale? | **Decided:** the AI register card with its three columns (as in V1), centred; the pause sits on it. |
